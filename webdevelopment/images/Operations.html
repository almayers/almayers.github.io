✨ Project Overview

This project is critical to success in this course.  The more time you spend planning NOW will make the rest of the course easier.  This is the #1 place students wish they would have spend more time and effort in this course.

Before building your website, it’s essential to plan your content, audience, and purpose. This assignment is the first step in developing your course-long web project. You’ll choose your website type, define your goals, and prepare your written content and media for use in future modules.

For this first assignment, you will focus specifically on planning the content of your website. While we will explore layout, design, and technical implementation in future modules, your task now is to consider the Key Questions in Website Design.

  • Who is your target audience?

  • What are the key features and functions of your site?

  • What is the overall look and feel you want?

  • What content will you include?

🛠️ About the Website Design Plan

Each week, you’ll build a new part of your personal website as we learn different web development skills. By the end of the course, you’ll have created a complete, fully functional, and customized website from scratch—designed and coded by you!

     🗂️ This assignment lays the foundation for everything that follows—take your time and think intentionally about your content!

🌐 Getting Started: Choose Your Website Type

You may choose one of the following website types:

  • Portfolio

  • Resume

  • Business

  • Educational

Each type has its own specific content and design expectations.
📎 Click here to view the Website Type Descriptions and Requirements (Google Drive) Links to an external site.
This document also includes a final project checklist Links to an external site.. You won’t need to understand everything just yet, but it will help guide your planning.

If you have an alternate website idea, contact your instructor for approval.

Your final website will include five fully coded and connected web pages. Each week, you’ll add new content and features as you learn more about HTML, CSS, and web design best practices.

✅ Project Requirements

Step 1: Choose Your Website Type

  • Select the website category (Portfolio, Resume, Business, or Educational).

  • Review the expectations Links to an external site. for each type to make sure you’ll have access to all the content you need for the course.

Step 2: Create a PDF Document (5 Sections Total)

Prepare a single PDF document with five separate sections—one for each section below:

a. Website Type & Purpose (Section 1)

  • Identify which type of website you will build (professional or educational).

  • Write a brief paragraph (max 150 words) explaining your choice and how this project aligns with your goals.

b. Home Page Content (Section 2)

  • Include all written content for your home page. (check the expectations for all content)

  • Think about how you will welcome your visitors and what message you want to communicate.

c. About / Educator Credentials Page (Section 3)

  • Include all written content for your "About" or "Credentials" page.

  • Add a professional photo of yourself (headshot recommended).

  • 🎥 Resume Video - Create a brief resume-style video (30 seconds–1 minute) introducing yourself.

You may:

  • Use a free tool like Powtoon

  • Record a video with your webcam, phone, or camera

  • Create a short PowerPoint or Google Slides video

Your video should briefly highlight who you are, your background, and how it connects to your chosen website topic.

📌 You can:

  • Save the video file for later upload

  • Or upload to a video-sharing platform (YouTube, Vimeo, etc.) and provide a link in your PDF

d. Projects/Experience OR Business/ Content Page (Section 4)

  • Include all written content and any images/artifacts you plan to feature.

  • You should already know what artifacts you will include and how they relate to your website’s purpose.

e. Reflection OR Resources (Page 5)

  • Include the final page content:

    • Reflection  for portfolio/ resume / educational sites

    • Resources  for business / educational sites

  • Follow the detailed project type guidelines provided on the Googledoc

  • Links to an external site...

Step 3: Prepare Your Resume

  • Create or update your CV/Resume or Business Flyer.

  • Save it as a PDF file using the format:
    [YourLastName]resume.pdf

Step 4: Save Your Work

  • Save both files to your  Webcontent folder on your desktop:

    • Your Website Planning PDF

    • Your Resume PDF

Keeping them organized in your folder now will make accessing them easier when we begin building your actual web pages.

📤 Submit

Upload both of the following files to this assignment:

  1. Website Planning PDF – All five sections, including the video link or placeholder.

  2. Resume PDF – Named: [YourLastName]resume.pdf

📌 Final Note

This content is your starting point. While you will continue refining and adjusting your pages as you develop them, this planning phase will save you time and help ensure your site is well-organized and complete by the end of the course.
