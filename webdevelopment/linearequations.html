Linear Equations

Students will solve one-variable linear equations using inverse operations and properties of equality.

What is a Linear Equation?

A linear equation is an equation where the highest power of the variable is 1.

Steps to Solve

  1. Identify the variable
  2. Use inverse operations
  3. Isolate the variable
  4. Check your answer
