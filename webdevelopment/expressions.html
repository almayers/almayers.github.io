Algebraic Expressions

Students will simplify algebraic expressions by combining like terms and applying order of operations.

What is an Expression?

An algebraic expression is a combination of variables, constants, and operations without an equal sign.

Key Parts

  • Variable: x, y
  • Coefficient: number in front of variable
  • Constant: number without variable
  • Term: each separated part of expression
