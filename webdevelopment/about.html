About Me

Amy Mayers - Mathematics Instructor

My name is Amy Mayers and I have been teaching Mathematics for about 16 years. With experience in both Middle School and High School math education, I have worked with both remedial courses as well as honors courses. My formal education and practical applications have created a level of expertise in both in person and virtual education for secondary math students.
