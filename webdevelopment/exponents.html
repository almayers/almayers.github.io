Exponents

Students will apply exponent rules to simplify expressions and understand repeated multiplication.

Rules of Exponents

  • Product Rule: x² · x³ = x⁵
  • Quotient Rule: x⁶ ÷ x² = x⁴
  • Power Rule: (x²)³ = x⁶
  • Zero Rule: x⁰ = 1
